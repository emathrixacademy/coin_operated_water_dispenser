Open items to settle before this is used for the build

  1. Gate B servo has no pin yet. Fabrication drawing Rev B replaces the single three-position diverter servo with two SG90 gate servos. The firmware pin map still lists one servo on D9. This sheet shows Gate A on D9 and Gate B as unallocated (dashed). The pin must be assigned by the Rev B work order and added to config.h and wiring.md together.
  2. Coin acceptor supply voltage. wiring.md puts the acceptor on the 5 V rail. Rev B names a CH-926 class acceptor, and that class commonly runs on 12 V. Confirm against the unit actually bought before wiring it.
  3. Coin box beam height. wiring.md says the beam sits at the fill line. Decision D-8 moved it to about 80% of the usable depth. The wiring is the same; the mounting position differs.
  4. Not specified anywhere yet: supply ratings, fuse values, wire gauges, connector types, LED resistor values, and which relay-board channel carries which load.
  5. Mains wiring (supply inlet, compressor leg, AC SSR) must be done and checked by a qualified electrician.

5. Build notes

  • Fit a flyback diode directly across the solenoid valve.
  • The pump needs a DC SSR or MOSFET module. An AC SSR will not switch DC.
  • Size the compressor SSR for at least three times the running current, mount it on a heatsink, and fit an RC snubber across the compressor.
  • Power both gate servos from the 5 V rail, not from the Mega's regulator. Gate A must rest on the hopper path with power off (Rev B, sheet 2).
  • Use a DS3231 module, not a DS1307 or DS3231M, with a good CR2032. If the module has a charging circuit, remove its resistor or fit an LIR2032.
  • Use the waterproof stainless DS18B20 probe.
  • Nextion TX goes to Mega RX2 (D17) and Nextion RX to Mega TX2 (D16), with a common ground.
  • Nothing uses D44–D46: the Servo library takes Timer5.
