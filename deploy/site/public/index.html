Water Refill Vending Machine

Built by eMathrix Technologies · Project EMX-2026-WATERVENDO-01

Research prototype. A coin-operated chilled-water kiosk: ₱1 buys 100 mL, up to ₱15 (1.5 L) per customer, with automatic change from ₱1 and ₱5 hoppers. Everything on this page matches the firmware and the as-built wiring of the unit on the bench.

3D simulation

Open the interactive 3D simulation
Operate the machine in the browser: insert coins, choose an amount on the same touchscreen the machine has, place a bottle and collect the change. Turn and zoom the cabinet to follow each coin through the gates and the water from the gallons to the bottle. Try the faults too: empty gallons, low change, a jammed hopper.

Firmware

Download the firmware (full source code)
Arduino Mega 2560 firmware (PlatformIO) and the ESP32 touchscreen firmware. The machine is standalone: no network, no cloud. Build and flash instructions are in the README inside the download.
