The rules behind it

RuleWhy
Coins decide the water; the water never decides the price.The price is taken from the credit before the pump starts, so a sensor's error can never change what a customer pays or the change they get.
₱1 = 100 mL, up to ₱20 (2 L) per customer.The ceiling bounds the change the hoppers must be able to pay.
Before taking a coin, the machine checks it could pay ₱39 of change (₱19, then a ₱20 coin).It never takes money it cannot honour. If it cannot, it shows "Not enough change in the machine".
No refund before buying.The machine sells water; it does not change coins (client decision, 31 Oct 2026).
A pour stopped early is charged in whole 100 mL steps, rounded down (305 mL counts as 300 mL).The rest of the price goes back to the credit in whole pesos, so the change can always be paid in coins.
Change is counted out coin by coin.A payout is only finished when every coin has been counted. If not, after three retries the machine records what it owes and locks.
