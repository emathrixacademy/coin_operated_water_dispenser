Wiring

Arduino Mega 2560 pin map, from include/config.h of the bench firmware (31 Oct 2026)

Check on the bench before rewiring. The firmware of 31 Oct changed three things that the as-built drawing of 29 Oct does not show yet: the coin slot pulse moved from D2 to D3 (and the flow input from D3 to D2), the dispense and fill pump relays swapped (dispense pump D32, fill pump D31), and the green CONFIRM button on D40 is now fitted. Rows marked changed 31 Oct are these. Confirm which way the unit is wired before flashing, so a coin is never read on the wrong pin.

The ESP32-3248S035C draws every screen; the Mega only sends short lines. The same board serves the phone page over its own WiFi hotspot (bench test feature, reaches a few metres).

Power

From the cabinet photos: a 12 V supply on the right-hand wall feeds a fused distribution block; a 12 V terminal strip and a 5 V terminal strip carry power to the pumps, hoppers, coin slot, sensors and the three 2-channel relay modules. The Mega sits on the left wall beside the coin slot. Details, fuse sizes and grounding are in the wiring notes.
