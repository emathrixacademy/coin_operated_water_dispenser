Customer touchscreen (as on the machine)

3.5" LCD · 480 × 320 · SIMULATION
Tap the screen as you would on the machine. It is drawn with the same layout, colours and words as the machine's display firmware. Hold the WATER VENDO title for 2 seconds, or tap Sales, to see today's sales.

Customer

₱1 buys 100 mL, up to ₱15 (1.5 L) per customer. After each coin the slot is closed for 1.3 s while the coin gates move; a coin dropped in then comes straight back to the tray, as on the machine.

What is happening

Machine readout

Service (attendant)

Admin or hold the big icon on the screen for 3 s
Admin asks for a 4-digit PIN (demo PIN 1234; each machine's owner sets their own under Change PIN). Three wrong PINs lock Admin for 5 minutes, and every login, wrong PIN and lockout is written to the history. Admin logs itself out after 60 s untouched, and opens only when no customer is mid-sale.
Inside: Profit (today and all time: money in, change paid out, profit, sales, water sold, change owed), Coins (₱1 / ₱5 / ₱10 / ₱20 inserted, what is in each hopper and the coin box, coins returned), Water (cold tank level, gallon A and B, refill pump), Load change, Clear fault, History, Change PIN.
Built from the specification (section 8) and this request; the machine firmware does not have these screens or the PIN yet.

The machine checks, before it takes a coin, that it can pay change for the worst case (₱34: ₱14 then a ₱20). If it cannot, it shows "Not enough change in the machine" and refuses money until change is loaded in Admin.
